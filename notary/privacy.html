Website & Inquiry Privacy Notice

Updated November 9, 2026

This notice covers the appointment inquiry and help desk forms on notary.worobi.com.

Information you submit

The form asks for your first and last name, email address, and requested service. You may also provide a phone number, preferred appointment date and time, and additional details. This information accompanies your request for a response about services and scheduling.

The help desk form asks for your name, email address, an issue summary, and details about your support request.

Form processing

When you submit either form, your entries are processed by this website and sent through Worobi Notary’s email service. Appointment inquiries go to notary@worobi.com for service follow-up. Help desk requests go to sos@worobi.com for support. Your name and email address are included so we can reply to you.

A form confirmation means the email server accepted the message for sending. It does not confirm inbox delivery, that a message has been read, or that an appointment is booked or changed.

Keep your inquiry brief

Please do not include passwords, Social Security numbers, payment-card details, identity-document numbers, medical information, or the contents of documents needing notarization. The inquiry form does not accept file uploads.

Questions

For questions about information submitted through this website, contact notary@worobi.com.
