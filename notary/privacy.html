Website & Inquiry Privacy Notice

Updated November 8, 2026

This notice covers the appointment inquiry form on notary.worobi.com.

Information you submit

The form asks for your first and last name, email address, and requested service. You may also provide a phone number, preferred appointment date and time, and additional details. This information accompanies your request for a response about services and scheduling.

Form processing

When you submit the form, your entries are sent to Formspree, the service used to process website inquiries.

Keep your inquiry brief

Please do not include Social Security numbers, payment-card details, identity-document numbers, medical information, or the contents of documents needing notarization. The inquiry form does not accept file uploads.

Questions

For questions about information submitted through this website, contact notary@worobi.com.
