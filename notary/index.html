Additional services by inquiry. Coordination and other project-based services are quoted individually based on the type and scope of the project. Availability, handling arrangements, and the total fee are confirmed before booking.

PRINTING & SCANNING

Paper and digital copies

Ask about printing or scanning needs, including page count, size, and required format. Equipment capability and secure transfer arrangements are confirmed before accepting files.

DOCUMENT PICKUP & DELIVERY

From one place to another

Ask about document transport between agreed locations. Timing, authorized recipients, handling, and any delivery confirmation requirements are agreed in advance.

APOSTILLE COORDINATION

Help coordinating the steps

Ask about assistance coordinating document submission and return for an apostille or authentication. Requirements depend on the document and destination.

Apostilles are issued by the appropriate government authority, not by the notary. Government fees and processing times are separate; approval and turnaround are not guaranteed. No legal advice is provided.
