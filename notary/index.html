Common Questions

Notary Package FAQ

Quick answers for remote online, mobile, real estate, estate, loan signing, apostille, and officiant appointments.

Do you offer remote online notarization?

Yes. Remote online notarization is available for eligible documents and signers through approved platforms. BlueNotary is the primary option, with Proof available as a backup when needed.

What do I need for a notarization?

Bring the unsigned document, every signer who needs notarization, and a valid government-issued photo ID. The signer must be alert, willing, and able to understand the document.

Why are packages more than the notary fee?

The Michigan notarial act fee is separate from platform, convenience, travel, witness, courier, printing, scanback, and coordination fees. The full total is confirmed before the appointment.

Can you handle estate or POA documents?

Yes, for attorney-prepared documents. I can help with the signing and notarization process, but I do not prepare legal documents or give legal advice.

Do you issue apostilles?

No. Apostilles are issued by the Michigan Office of the Great Seal. I can help with notarization review, prep, mailing or courier coordination, and status support.

Are you available for weddings?

Yes. Wedding officiant services are available as an extra service for simple signings, short ceremonies, vow renewals, and local civil ceremonies.
